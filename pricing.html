Frequently asked questions

What is Motive?

Motive helps friends find and rank food places based on everyone's preferences.

How does Motive work?

Create a blend, invite your friends, set your preferences, and discover a ranked list of places that suit your group.

Can I choose my budget?

Yes. You can set your budget and other preferences to help find places that work for your group.

Can I use Motive with my friends?

Yes. Invite your friends using a link and combine everyone's preferences to find places together.
